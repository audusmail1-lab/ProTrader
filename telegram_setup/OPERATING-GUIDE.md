## How support works

Visitors → bot → name, category, device/browser, short issue and optional screenshot → review/submit → private support record plus your Telegram inbox. You answer using the commands below. The person receives the reply in the same bot chat.

Telegram requests are separate from the teaching dashboard. Applications still need instructor approval; lesson questions stay in the Academy classroom. The bot never approves enrollment or trades for visitors.

### Owner commands — send privately to the bot

- `/tickets` — show the latest 20 open support requests.
- `/reply 12 Your reply here` — answer request 12.
- `/close 12` — close a resolved request.
- `/delivery` — check queued, sent or failed deliveries.

Use `/support` for a new follow-up and include the previous ticket number. Check delivery failures before assuming a reply reached someone.
