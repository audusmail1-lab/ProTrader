02 · How it works
Learn

One idea per lesson

Charts, the trade ticket, the app's analysis and a full practice workflow. Plain language, a short video, and an exercise you do yourself.

Practice

The same screen, live prices

Open the terminal and do what the lesson described: mark the level, read the setup check, fill in the ticket. Paper money, real market prices.

Review

Every trade is on the record

Closed trades are logged with entry, exit, result and how they closed — stop, target or by hand — so you review what you did, not what you remember.
