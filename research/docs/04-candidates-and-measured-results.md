# Ranked candidates and measured results (5 Oct 2026)

Two parts, kept apart on purpose: **A** what was measured (development evidence on one year of Deriv history, estimated spreads, no commission/swap/slippage), **B** what is proposed next. Nothing in A is forward evidence; the window (3 Oct 2025 → 5 Oct 2026) overlaps the year the 7.2c filters and the four "A" slices were chosen on, so any per-slice positive here is **in-sample** for those choices. The only clean evidence for the incumbent is the forward paper journal, which the review loop now counts.

Working-strategy criteria and the placebo rule were written before any run (`research/hypotheses.json`, loaded into the registry; shown by `GET /api/sentinel/research/hypotheses`). Every experiment: `python research_lab.py report <id>`; raw results `research/experiments/<id>.result.json`.

## A. Measured

**Setup.** 16 slices (8 focus markets × 15m, 1h; H6 uses 1h, 4h), chronological windows: development 3 Oct 2025 → 4 Apr 2026, validation → 17 Jun 2026, holdout → 5 Oct 2026. The holdout is reused across all seven experiments (logged; 93 configurations tried in total, so a single p < 0.05 anywhere is not evidence). Costs at ×1, ×1.5, ×2 of the estimates. Placebo = accepted subset vs 1,000 random subsets of equal size drawn from the base run's signals, on net and gross R (post #3977's test).

**Baseline (ARIA 7.1 / 7.2c, the incumbent).** 6,257 trades, **−0.035 R/trade** [−0.070, −0.001], PF 0.94, gross (before spread) +0.041; development −0.041, validation −0.024, holdout −0.036; at ×1.5 spreads −0.074, at ×2 −0.112. Every window negative after estimated costs. The 4h-trend filter earns its place (removing it: −0.044 on 11,376 trades, worse in all three windows) and the trailing exit beats the fixed 2.5R target (−0.075 on 9,715). Per slice, NAS100 15m (+0.19, n 305) and XAU 15m (+0.14, n 511) are positive in all three windows and USDJPY 15m/1h negative in all three — the same pattern the 28 Sep selection saw on nearly the same data, so this confirms nothing new; the forward journal's A-grade result (n 16, −0.43 R) is the evidence that counts.

**Cost sensitivity, all variants.** At ×1.5 estimated spreads nothing is above −0.02 R in any window; at ×2 everything is clearly negative. Post #4114 measured live XAUUSD spreads about three times the bar-field median; until Deriv MT5 costs are sampled (blocked input), the ×1.5 column is the prudent reading.

**Summary of A.** Seven hypotheses, 58 configurations, one year, three windows: **no validated edge found.** The incumbent's own filters (4h trend, trailing exit) are the only components that demonstrably help, and only relative to worse versions of itself. The no-trade state is legitimate and remains the default position of the research record.

## B. Proposed (not measured, nothing adopted)

B1. **Keep the incumbent cohort counting; read its 100/150 reviews first.** On launch the loop produces the 7.2c diagnostic (100) and formal (150) reviews from the forward journal; the diagnosis (entry / regime / session / exit / costs / uncertainty, each in R) decides which library rows to draw from, not the shortlist order.

B2. **Adopt H2 as a cost rule only, after measured spreads exist.** Proposed rule: skip entries with measured spread / stop > 0.05 (cap bounds 0.04–0.08 all behave the same). It cannot be counted as edge; it can stop the book paying a fifth of the stop to enter 15m FX.

B3. **Add two shadows to the incumbent** (10-bar counter-channel exit; 2×ATR ratchet trail) so the forward journal collects paired exit evidence at no cost; judge at the 150-shadow boundary with the pre-registered paired rule (H4's rejection criteria). This is a code change to `sentinel_core.shadow_rules()` and is not in this change set.

B4. **Measure costs** (`POST /api/sentinel/costs` samples by hour; commission and swap from the MT5 spec) and re-run H1–H7 at measured spreads. If measured spreads are wider than the estimates, every number in A gets worse; if narrower on the index/gold slices in session, H1 deserves a second look *with the same rejection criteria*.

B5. **Next two challengers**, only if the incumbent's 150-review names the factor: entry quality → H7's Fib/Stoch-required child as a *paper* version (it is the only thing in A at ≈ 0 R net); exit → B3's shadows; costs → B2; regime → nothing (H3/H5 closed); session → nothing (H1 closed). Two components at a time, each a new immutable version with its own cohort.

B6. **Out of scope until data exists:** news blackout (L06), gold reopen study (H12, needs a reopen-cost sample), ML meta-label (H11, deferred on the channel's own null results), the D1 templates (L19).

## C. Known limits of A

Estimated spreads (one number per market, no hour-of-day); no commission, swap, slippage, execution delay; stop-first on contested bars; one feed (Deriv public WebSocket, the same bars the app charts); trades treated as independent in the normal intervals (the block-bootstrap intervals in the registry's reviews address this); the one-year window contains one regime set; development and validation were used to choose nothing here, but the holdout has now been seen 93 times and is no longer untouched for any of these ideas.
