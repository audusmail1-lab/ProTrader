What breakeven saves, and what it costs

Against simply holding to the stop or the 2.5R target, every rule saves R on trades that would have lost and gives up R on trades that would have won. Its value is the difference.

Every rule tested

Full loss = closed at −1R. Protected = stop reached breakeven or better at some point. Kept of peak = exit ÷ best open profit, on trades that reached +1R.
